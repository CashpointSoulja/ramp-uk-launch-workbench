# 5 Whys, jobs to be done and metrics

Independent concept by Ayo Ahmed. Everything below is a working hypothesis built from public sources, not interviews or internal data.

## 5 Whys

**Starting problem:** early UK outreach could be generic, mistimed or wrong about what the product does in the UK.

1. **Why?** Drafts get written from a company name and a general pitch rather than from what the company has said in public.
2. **Why?** The public evidence (a job post, a launch note) isn't captured in a form that keeps what it says apart from what we infer.
3. **Why?** Research, drafting and checking happen in different places, and the checks (customer status, UK claims) depend on memory.
4. **Why?** A new market has no settled list of claims, so home-market language, such as US banking terms or VAT handling, can slip in unnoticed.
5. **Why?** Nothing turns a field learning ("we can't answer the German entity question") into an owned question for product.

**Root cause, as a hypothesis:** there is no single, small workflow that keeps evidence, inference and unknowns apart, forces the customer check and claim review, and routes learnings back.

## Jobs to be done

- **Functional:** when a company posts a public sign that it is setting up its UK finance function, I want to turn that into a brief with the evidence attached and a draft I can check, so that any outreach is timely and accurate.
- **Safety:** when I'm about to copy a draft, I want to know the customer status was checked and the claims match UK pages, so that I don't contact an existing customer or overpromise.
- **Learning:** when a prospect question has no clear answer, I want to log it with an owner, so that product and positioning can decide rather than the question getting lost.
- **Emotional:** feel confident that what goes out is defensible, line by line.
- **Social:** be seen by product and legal colleagues as careful with claims in a new market.

## Metrics (proposed, not measured)

None of these have been measured, and no targets are set. A baseline would come first. The desk itself records nothing about usage. Each count would come from a person tallying exported briefs and feedback CSVs over a trial period.

| Type | Metric | How it would be counted |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome candidate | Share of first replies that engage with the stated reason for contact | Manual tally from the team's own inbox, outside the desk |
| Leading | Time from public signal logged to reviewed brief | Check date in the brief vs. the export timestamp |
| Leading | Briefs with every unknown either resolved or logged as feedback | Count from exported briefs and the feedback CSV |
| Quality guardrail | Drafts that needed a claim correction at review | Reviewer note per exported brief |
| Safety guardrail | Outreach attempts to existing customers | Should be zero; recorded by the team if it happens |
| Learning | Feedback items that reach a decision other than "New" | Feedback CSV, Decision column |
| Freshness | Briefs exported more than 30 days after the source check date | Exported brief header |
