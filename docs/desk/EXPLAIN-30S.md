# UK launch desk in 30 seconds

**What:** one page that turns a public signal into a reviewed UK account brief.

**How:**

1. Paste in what a public page says: who, the link, the facts, your hypothesis and what you don't know.
2. The desk builds a brief, discovery questions and a role-only draft from a fixed template. Same input, same output.
3. You check customer status yourself. Unknown or existing customer locks everything.
4. Five review checks unlock copy or export. Change the draft, the facts or the status, and the checks reset.
5. What you couldn't answer becomes an owned question for product, positioning or targeting.

**What it doesn't do:** use AI, scrape, connect to a CRM, track anyone or send anything. Keyword checks are prompts, not compliance.

**Where the data lives:** in your browser, on this device. Back it up or clear it from the footer.

**Status:** an independent concept by Ayo Ahmed, not affiliated with Ramp. Sources checked 27 Oct 2026. It is a working prototype of a workflow hypothesis, not a production tool.
