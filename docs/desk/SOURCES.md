## Content rules drawn from the sources

- Ramp captures and codes VAT; the desk never says it files VAT returns or reclaims VAT.
- US banking or deposit language isn't used for the UK offer.
- Whether offices or entities outside the UK are eligible is treated as unknown.
- Multi-entity and multi-currency are presented as Plus features.
- No savings figures, customer results, market sizes or response rates appear anywhere.

## Screened out

Fyxer and ElevenLabs appear in UK launch materials as Ramp customers (source 2). They are listed as text for audit only and never as prospects.
