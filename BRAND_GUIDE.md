# UK Launch Desk: brand reference

Independent concept by Ayo Ahmed. Captured 27 October 2026 from Ramp's public UK pages. This is a reference study, not official brand guidance or an affiliation claim.

## Observed

- Literal wordmark: viewBox `0 0 71 20`; captured display size 75 × 20px. The SVG bytes are retained unchanged in `ramp-wordmark.svg`.
- Primary action colour: `#e4f222`; captured dark button `#1a1919`. Hero text `#0c0a08` is distinct from the button colour.
- Declared type family: Lausanne, Arial, sans-serif. Public site declares weights 300, 350, 400 and 700. No font binaries are bundled in this concept; Arial is the available fallback.
- Desktop captured hero: 64px / 64px, weight 400. Section heading: 40px / 42px. Hero paragraph: 20px / 26px at 60% opacity.
- Header controls: 6px corner radius. Hero action: 52px high, 8px radius, 20px horizontal padding. Email input: 52px high, 10px radius. Hero form: 500px wide.
- Art language: physical card and phone objects; receipt and approval layers; pale neutral and dotted backgrounds; thin connector lines; small anchoring dots; editorial headings and generous space.

## Concept choices

The workbench uses the literal logo and three first-party artwork captures, with source URLs and file hashes in `ART_SOURCES.json`. The 48px concept section headings, mobile 40px hero, responsive breakpoints, panels and workflow controls are design choices. They are not described as Ramp's exact production measurements.

Mobile production typography was not independently confirmed. The earlier 14px / 19.6px paragraph measurement was not established as hero copy and is not used as such.

## UK content boundaries

The launch joins cards, expenses, supplier payments and accounting workflows. Re-check live UK product and pricing pages before making a claim. Do not copy US deposit, banking or savings language into a UK draft. VAT capture/coding is not VAT filing or reclaim. Non-UK entity eligibility remains a question rather than a promise.
