                    How to read this desk

                    Observed means it appears in a linked source. Hypothesis means it’s our reading of that source. Unknown means nobody here has checked it yet.

                    Who it’s for: someone planning UK go-to-market work from public signals, checking a company before any outreach. It doesn’t fetch pages, use AI, connect to a CRM or send anything. Keyword checks catch a few risky phrases; they aren’t a compliance review.

                    Feedback items and the outreach draft are samples.

                    Saved on this device

                    Records, drafts, review checks and feedback are saved in this browser’s local storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and there’s no account.
